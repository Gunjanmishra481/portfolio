The problem

Navigation apps optimise for time. None of them tell you what a route costs in emissions, so the greener choice stays invisible.

What I built

  1. 01Estimates each route's CO₂ emissions using DEFRA/EEA emission factors.
  2. 02Ranks routes with a multi-criteria weighted-sum model.
  3. 03Surfaces the greener option right next to the fastest one.

Outcome

Lower-emission routes become a one-tap choice instead of guesswork.
