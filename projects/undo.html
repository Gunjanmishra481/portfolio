The problem

Send money to a mistyped UPI ID and getting it back means complaints, follow-ups and waiting — with nobody guiding you through the process.

What I built

  1. 01An AI agent that takes a wrong-transfer report and drives the recovery process.
  2. 02Designed as a partner integration that plugs into Paytm and banks.
  3. 03An installable PWA that works on any phone.
  4. 04Hosted on AWS and integrated with the Paytm Payment Gateway sandbox.

Outcome

A working recovery flow, from reporting the mistake to following up on it, in one place.
