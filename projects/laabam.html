The problem

What I built

  1. 01Snap a photo of a purchase bill to record it.
  2. 02Bill customers by voice instead of typing.
  3. 03See real profit without doing any bookkeeping.

Outcome

Profit visibility for merchants who would never open a spreadsheet.
