The problem

Every trip the business ran was logged by hand in a monthly Excel loading sheet — trailer, driver, LR number, route, cars carried, diesel, POD, trip allowance, revenue and dealer. Keeping it current and raising invoices from it was slow, manual work.

What I built

  1. 01A trip and load log the operations person fills in from a phone, tablet or desktop.
  2. 02Touch-first forms and cards instead of spreadsheet cells — no keyboard-only workflows.
  3. 03Client invoices generated in the business's existing format: invoice sheet plus VIN details.
  4. 04Issuer details built in; client details stay editable.

Outcome

Replaced the monthly Excel loading sheet and cut the client's manual spreadsheet work.
